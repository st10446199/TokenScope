02 / How it works

Inspect a compact JWT header, payload and time claims. Expiry uses your device clock and is evaluated when you click Analyze.

Decoding does not verify a signature. This tool cannot tell you whether a token is authentic or authorised.

Use synthetic tokens when sharing screenshots. Export includes the decoded claims. No inputs are stored.
